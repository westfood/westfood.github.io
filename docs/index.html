WAYPOINT TECH INC. / Startup

Feb 2026–Now

Owner of Agent-first DevOps & Cloud Engineering, Agent Operations

Waypoint was my ticket to experience agent-driven product delivery first-hand. I took over the CTO’s Cloud & DevOps responsibilities and gradually embraced an agent-driven approach to all aspects of my work.

While providing evidence for the SOC 2 Audit I was exploring ways to automatically align our compliance via Vanta MCP just by developing merge request skills. I established Docker Sandbox to restrict agents’ access to cloud credentials and as one of the agent sandboxes for the product’s dynamic pipeline capability. I scoped permissions for agents to perform routine root-cause analysis in the cloud, performed agent-driven migration of production environments between hyperscalers and code bases. I used agents in E2B platform delivery, Terraform development and all other aspects of continuous delivery.

MSD, Czech Republic / Enterprise

Jun 2020–Jan 2026

ML Platform Engineer → Data Science Platform Delivery Lead → Data Science Platforms Product Line Architect

I was hired as Big Data Platform Engineer for Hadoop deployments in AWS. I started to work on an in-house, AWS-native Machine Learning Platform and within the first year I became delivery lead of a Data Science platform powered by Dataiku. I stood up a platform with my team from nothing to an established enterprise platform providing Data Science, Data Processing, Machine Learning and AI-driven capabilities. By continuous improvement and efficient delivery, we spearheaded emerging enterprise enablers such as MCS 2.0 AWS Accounts, Kubernetes and enterprise-aligned Terraform Modules. We delivered Karpenter-powered scheduling and drove integration with the Enterprise Agent Platform.

As Product Line Architect I was responsible for technical evaluation and delivery of new capabilities. I delivered Code Ocean and Domino Data Science Platform for evaluation by Research Laboratories. Once Domino was chosen to power one of the analytical components in a new Clinical Pipeline, I worked closely with MRL & MRL IT to deliver a Business Critical System with my team.

In collaboration with AI Compliance and DevSecOps teams, we provided a Hugging Face model ingestion gateway, enabling scanning of models and storing them in Artifactory. We exposed onboarded models to the community via an Enterprise AI asset catalog. Meanwhile I was driving engagement with Enterprise Architecture to integrate our product line with the emerging Agentic Platform.

ANGELCAM, INC. / Startup

Oct 2017–May 2020

Owner of Cloud Engineering & Operations, DevOps

My responsibility was maintaining and improving operations, documentation, deployments, monitoring and security of services deployed across different infrastructure providers. Angelcam operates Cloud Recording, Streaming and Broadcasting services integrated with IoT sensors to provide a real-time security service for customers via mobile and web-based interfaces. I ran our operations across AWS, Digital Ocean and multiple bare-metal providers.

Among the initial deliverables was containerisation of Rust and Python-based services, their continuous delivery and autoscaling in Docker Swarm. I moved observability and alerting to Prometheus and exposed metrics to the team with Grafana. A big part of the job was building trust with customers, as it depended on reliable connectivity to AWS. In the security market, each downtime in recording might be a reason for cancelling a subscription. I made sure uptime of different services was not just high but also transparent and publicly observable.

NATIONAL LIBRARY OF THE CZECH REPUBLIC

Feb 2011–Sep 2017

Owner of Web Archiving Operation → Head of Application Support

My job was to deliver and continuously improve web archiving services for the National Library. Early in my career I had the opportunity to participate in a staff exchange at the Internet Archive in San Francisco. Among other things, this helped me understand configuration implications and behaviour of their archival crawler and kick-started my knowledge about how huge amounts of data are stored and processed in a meaningful way.

I designed and implemented recurrent Czech domain and topical crawls. I maintained a couple of on-premises storage servers with new hard drives, including a couple of RAID 6 resurrections. I was operating an archival retrieval service, our Web Archive websites and other services, research projects and prototypes in vSphere or on bare metal. Before I left I took responsibility for the Application Services Department, as I wanted to drive technical collaboration inside the library and across Czech and foreign libraries.

DARK CIVILISATIONS / Computer Game

Dec 2025–Now

Dark Factory Development in Rust & Bevy

My studying of human civilisation and playing games formed my ideas on approaching the Civilisation genre. With recent improvements in LLMs and agentic tools I was able to deliver prototype components beyond my programming, academic and mathematical skills. I embrace a dark factory approach and focus on iterative specification of game systems and developing visual appeal.

To help agents resolve technical issues, I usually instruct them to conceptualise simulation and computations for me. This greatly simplified navigating technical decisions affecting performance and behaviour of the simulation. Driving artistic aspects of the game, such as music and art, is way more tricky, but I am slowly getting better at adopting what generative AI can do well.

This project helps me greatly in assessing capabilities of frontier models and their quirks. I found it very efficient to dedicate a server to the agents and interact with them remotely. Without the recent rise of AI capabilities I would never be able to make any meaningful progress as a salary man.
